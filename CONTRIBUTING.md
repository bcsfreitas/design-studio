# Contributing

Design Studio is in early beta. You get the source code, and you're encouraged to customize your own studio to fit your needs.

Share what you build, ask questions, and offer feedback in the [GitHub community](https://github.com/itspatmorgan/design-studio/discussions).

If you find a bug, you can also [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/itspatmorgan/design-studio/issues).
